Settings guide

Best OBS Studio settings for Windows — streaming & recording guide 2026

The best OBS settings for Windows depend on your GPU. For NVIDIA: use NVENC H.264 encoder, 6000 kbps bitrate for 1080p60 streaming. For recording use CQP 18–20 with NVENC for near-lossless quality.

FAQ

Settings questions

Best OBS settings for streaming on Windows — NVENC or x264?

Always prefer NVENC (NVIDIA), AMF (AMD) or QSV (Intel) hardware encoders over x264 when available. Hardware encoding uses the GPU encoder chip, leaving CPU free for gaming. x264 uses CPU and causes frame drops in games. Only use x264 if your GPU does not have a hardware encoder or its quality is insufficient.

What bitrate for 1080p60 streaming on Twitch?

Twitch recommends 6000 kbps for 1080p60. With Twitch Partner or Turbo, you can go up to 8000 kbps. For YouTube Live, 8000–12000 kbps is recommended for 1080p60. For stable streams on slower connections, lower to 4500 kbps for 1080p30.
